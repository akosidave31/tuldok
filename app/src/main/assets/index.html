The tracing games check that children start at the green dot and follow the arrow, the way letters are taught in class. Nursery gets wider paths and big letters only. Each age group sees the games that suit it: First sounds, Rhymes and Add it up start at Pre-K; First words starts at Kinder. After two wrong taps, the right answer glows.
